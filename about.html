Who We Are

Welcome to Cycle Mart

Cycle Mart is a bicycle shop dedicated to providing quality bicycles for riders looking for reliable, stylish and affordable bikes.

We specialize in ex-UK bicycles, offering customers a variety of bikes suitable for commuting, fitness, recreation and everyday cycling.

Our goal is to make it easier for customers in Nakuru and beyond to find bicycles that match their riding needs and budget.
